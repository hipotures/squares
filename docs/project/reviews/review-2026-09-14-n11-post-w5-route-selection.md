# Post-W5 route selection for material small-n progress

## Decision

Select Route A at side $q=96/25=3.84$ for the next scientific program, but separate its
admission from its target run.

The immediate entry is BC-354 / `think-0t5y`: a 75-minute, no-target block that must
freeze one complete same-corner availability-blocker root, its physical transfer,
matched baseline, conditional capacities, exact checker, controls, and kill rule.
Only after that PR merges does BC-341 / `think-9y6q` run one exact discriminator.

If BC-354 cannot admit the complete root and checker inside its block, stop Route A at
the representation boundary and select Route S / BC-343 as the sole fallback.
Route S must freeze its complexity metric before optimization; a literal fivefold
reduction of T-025’s 119 independent $D_4$ orbit representatives means at most 23
representatives.

No scientific target, solver, search, or verifier ran in this planning block.
The frontier remains

$$
3.826447410572939\ldots \le s(11) \le 3.877083590022814\ldots.
$$

## Route A admission contract

### Physical denominator

The retained selection reduction leaves 16 ordered availability-blocker products:

- four same-corner products;
- eight adjacent-corner products;
- four opposite-corner products.

Subdividing a root into pose, label, incidence, or source-cell leaves does not increase
the denominator. If the fixed left/bottom chart from S1 is used, its bookkeeping has ten
symmetry types; it cannot silently inherit the three physical $D_4$ types.

BC-354 freezes one complete same-corner root, represented by

$$
0,15\notin A_{\mathrm{BL}}(P),
$$

with all co-ownership, split-ownership, missing-mark, label, continuous angle, seam, and
contact cases retained.
A complete closure transfers by global $D_4$ symmetry to the other three same-corner
products. It does not close the adjacent or opposite roots.

### Shared physical variables

Every residual placement and parent constraint must use one coherent physical parent.
Independent compatibility of several owners is not simultaneous compatibility.
For an incidence stratum with $\sigma$ additional forced marks, retain $k=4+\sigma$
distinct mark owners and residual requirement $11-k=7-\sigma$.

The admission must state:

1. the complete root domain and its boundary convention;
2. the strict-core transfer used on every subdomain;
3. the unchanged point-resource baseline;
4. each candidate conditional threshold or subset-capacity row;
5. an exact whole-domain checker and a method-distinct coverage replay;
6. the original-root denominator, closed roots, and exact worst surviving domain.

H-155 remains `instrument_ready: false`. The existing threshold tools certify the whole
centre domain and $D_4$ transport; `multi_owner_domains.py` constructs residual polygons
separately. BC-354 must join those objects with a shared-variable forbidden-region and
budget contract. Closing one raw owner tuple is useful local evidence, but is not one of
the 16 physical roots.

### Controls and verdicts

The positive control is the unchanged baseline on the exact same domain.
The adversarial controls include the four literal corner parents retained by T1/H-161,
whose current owner surplus and parent-union charges do not remove their residual
extensions.

- **Admit:** both exact coverage routes accept the complete frozen domain and every
  candidate row has a proved budget below the residual requirement.
- **Reject the candidate row:** a legal exact witness satisfies the candidate resources.
- **Park the representation:** an exact witness survives every frozen resource under
  refinement, or the shared-variable adapter cannot cover the root completely.
- **Unresolved:** timeout, incomplete enumeration, or an unclassified seam.
  None is a mathematical refutation.
