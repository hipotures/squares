## Post-W5 Mathematical Strategy

The primary strategy is to cross the known point-and-density ceiling with information
that comes from physical compatibility rather than finer sampling of the same
relaxation. Route A does this most directly: freeze one complete occupancy or contact
root family at `3.84`, preserve its original denominator, and ask whether proved
conditional capacities close the family.
`3.85` is not interchangeable with `3.84`; it requires the owner and capacity premises
to be rebuilt.

If a complete Route A admission slice cannot be frozen within one bounded block, Route S
should take precedence.
Its exact end-to-end verifier already exists, so it offers a realistic path to a much
simpler proof of `s(11) >= 3.82` even though it does not improve the numerical frontier.
Route E is the cheapest subsequent mechanism screen because its angular-count inputs are
already proved; it still needs a sound coherent-resource consumer before it can support
a global certificate.

Routes B, F1, and F2 remain the most interesting ways to change the relaxation itself,
but their first blocks must pay for soundness or admission rather than claim a bound.
The `n = 12` route has theorem-sized upside but no instrument-ready uniform lemma.
Orientation, constructive search, and geometric waste stay behind their stated controls.
