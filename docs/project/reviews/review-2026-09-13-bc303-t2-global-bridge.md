## The Quantifiers That Still Matter

Fix $K=[0,q]^2$, $q=96/25$, and one coherent admitted equipment choice $E$ for a
physical packing $P$. Its unit parents $Q_i$ have disjoint interiors, and its closed
selected cores $C_i\subset\operatorname{int}Q_i$ have half-side $h=9977/20000$. Physical
parent angles range over their full admitted source cells.
Core membership, mark ownership, and angular label bins are closed.

In the bottom-left chart the marks are $m_1=(a,b)$ and $m_2=(b,a)$, where $a=3152/3175$
and $b=2336/3175$. The other corners use $I,H,V,HV$, with $I$ the identity,
$H(x,y)=(q-x,y)$, and $V(x,y)=(x,q-y)$. Write $J(x,y)=(-y,x)$. A proper signed core
frame is $(e,Je)$, with $e$ one of the four oriented core axes.
It supplies the label $8(a'-1)+j$ for owned mark $m_{a'}$ when both coordinates of the
mark-to-centre vector in that frame are nonnegative and the angle of $e$ lies in the
closed bin $[j\pi/4,(j+1)\pi/4]$, modulo $2\pi$. All such frames and bins contribute
labels.

At corner $c$, let $A_c(P,E)$ contain every label supplied by the actual owners and all
permitted proper signed frames.
Put $V_c=A_c\cap\{0,15\}$. A forced-0 corner has $V_c=\{0\}$, and a forced-15 corner has
$V_c=\{15\}$. Role C has one owner of both marks; role S has two distinct owners, one
per mark; an O role owns one mark and leaves the other missing.
Missing in a local fixture does not establish globally unowned in an eventual packing.
The imported seven-mark rule allows at most one globally unowned mark.
[Definitions and transfer][routing]

For the two admitted tuple geometries $G_0=\{(0,0,0,0),(15,15,15,15)\}$, routing is
exactly

\[
\bigl(\prod_c A_c(P,E)\bigr)\cap G_0\ne\varnothing.
\]

This is equivalent to every $V_c$ being nonempty and no pair being opposing singletons.
T2 concerns the second condition.
T1’s counterexample concerns a sufficient test for the first.
The Cartesian product chooses among owners already present in one packing; it cannot
combine independent geometric witnesses.

This routing proof of physical exclusion needs
\(\forall P\;\exists E\;[\text{routing holds for }(P,E)]\). The documented theorem over
all equipped packings is a stronger sufficient formulation.

displayed statement.

## Prospective Deduction 1: Account for All Eleven Core Floors

Let $\mu$ be the frozen [BC293 measure][measure], a nonnegative point measure on 377
sites in $K$, with total mass $M$. Each of the eight marks has weight $w$. The exact
source constants are

\[
W=4000000,\quad M=\frac{22524199}{2000000},\quad
g=\frac3{800000},\quad w=\frac{106251}{800000},\quad
\varepsilon=M-11.
\]

The imported floor is $\mu(C_i)\ge1+g$. Define the nonnegative excess
\(\beta_i=\mu(C_i)-(1+g)\), unused point mass \(U=M-\sum_{i=1}^{11}\mu(C_i)\), and
remaining allowance

\[
\Delta=\varepsilon-11g=\frac{1048233}{4000000}.
\]

Disjoint closed cores give the exact identity

\[
U+\sum_{i=1}^{11}\beta_i=\Delta.
\]

If a selected pair of corner roles contains $k$ distinct owners and $u\in\{0,1\}$
globally unowned marks, then $U\ge uw$. All omitted $\beta_i$ are nonnegative, so every
extension must satisfy

\[
\sum_{i\in I}\beta_i\le\Delta-uw.
\tag{1}
\]

Equivalently its ordinary owner surplus is at most \(\varepsilon-uw-(11-k)g\). X-029
already states this necessary extension inequality; the new deduction is its complete
C/S reduction and integer decision thresholds.

Let $B_*$ be the minimum of \(\sum_{i\in X}\beta_i\) over every full forced-0 local
configuration $X$, including both actual C and actual S domains from X-029. S requires
joint physical parents.
The minimum exists: the domains are nonempty, only finitely many atomic membership sets
occur, and C/S have fixed owner counts one/two.

Consider the *separate strengthened helper* requiring strict violation of (1) on every
admitted adjacent/opposite opposing-type pair.
In this local helper’s domain, $u$ is the role’s declared missing-mark count.
Applying the helper to a packing uses that packing’s actual global incidence.
Then

\[
\boxed{\text{the strengthened pair helper holds}\iff B_*>\Delta/2.}
\tag{2}
\]

**Proof.** Diagonal reflection exchanges forced types and preserves measure, equipment,
and owner count. Every full/full pair therefore has excess at least $2B_*$. A
one-missing/full pair has excess at least $B_*$, because the O owner’s excess is
nonnegative. Here

\[
2Ww=1062510>1048233=W\Delta,
\]

so $B_*>\Delta/2>\Delta-w$. The two-missing role is absent.
These inequalities prove the forward sufficiency for all adjacent and opposite
realizations. Conversely, an attaining full corner with $B_*\le\Delta/2$ and its
diagonally reflected opposite copy have excess $2B_*\le\Delta$. X-029’s proved parent
separation

\[
2q-2(a+b)-4=708/3175>0
\]

makes this an actual opposite pair, contradicting the strengthened helper.
This local pair still need not extend to eleven parents.
All statements use the same closed, symmetric equipment domain; no deterministic
endpoint tie selector is substituted.

The integer arithmetic is

\[
F=W(1+g)=4000015,\qquad WM=45048398,\qquad
WM-11F=1048233.
\]

Thus integer excess must be at least $524117$, since $2(524116)<1048233<2(524117)$.

| Complete or sufficient test | Original required lower bound | Strengthened criterion |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Complete C minimum | $W\mu(C)\ge4524200$ | $W\mu(C)\ge4524132$ |

| S first-owner sufficient minimum | $W\mu(C_1)\ge4524185$ | $W\mu(C_1)\ge4524132$ |

The endpoint checks against the full eleven-parent budget are explicit:

\[
\begin{aligned}

2(8524146)+7F&=45048397, &2(8524147)+7F&=45048399.

For the sufficient S filter, $4524132+F=8524147$. The improvement is 68 integer units

## Prospective Deduction 2: H161 Persists Under Small Axis Translations

H161’s closed-parent scan has no captured atom on $\partial Q_0$. Any source atom on
that boundary would be captured, so the entire finite support avoids it.
T1’s captured core atoms are all strictly interior, and a point excluded from a closed
core cannot be on its boundary.
Thus the support also avoids $\partial C_0$, where

\[
C_0=[23/20000,19977/20000]^2.
\]

For each source atom, membership in either translated square is constant for all
sufficiently small translations: an interior point retains positive edge slacks and an
exterior point retains a violated edge inequality.
Intersect these finitely many translation neighborhoods.
Some $\eta_0>0$ preserves all 377 membership decisions in both squares.
This is an existence proof from retained incidence facts, not a new scan or a numerical
radius claim. [H161 incidence][h161], [T1 strictness][t1]

Choose

\[
0<\eta<\min\{\eta_0,\ b-1/2,\ (q-2)/2\},
\qquad b-1/2=1497/6350>0.
\]

For every $0\le u,v<\eta$, take $Q_{u,v}=Q_0+(u,v)$ and $C_{u,v}=C_0+(u,v)$, with their
unchanged axis rays.
Then:

- The unit parent stays in $K$, its axis source chart remains admitted, and the core
  stays strictly inside it with the same inset $23/20000$.
- Both marks remain in the core.
  The centre coordinates $1/2+u,1/2+v$ remain below $b<a$, so all mark-to-centre
  coordinates are negative and the complete labels remain $\{3,4,11,12\}$.
- Parent and core retain exactly their 19 source atoms and integer mass $F$.

Transport any four independently chosen such configurations by the corner maps
$I,H,V,HV$. Parents at different corners remain separated in at least one coordinate by
at least $q-2-2\eta>0$. Weighted D4 invariance preserves each mass; complete corner
labels are obtained by transporting the proper frames and corner charts.
Consequently this family still co-owns all eight marks, lacks both certified types at
each corner, and has parent-union mass $4F$. Its necessary extension account is

\[
4F+7F=44000165<45048398,
\]

with the same $1048233$ units of slack.
It is a relative neighborhood within the fixed-axis admissible translation stratum.
It makes no claim about S1-normalized translations, arbitrary physical angles, a
specified radius, or eleven-parent extendibility.
Excluding these configurations needs information beyond this particular parent-union
mass and the omitted core floors.
Other parent poses and other measures remain separate possibilities.
