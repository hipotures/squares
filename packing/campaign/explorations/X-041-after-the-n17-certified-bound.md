## Question 1: Is the Covering-LP Experiment Still the Right Next Move?

The question as the record states it (`RESULTS.md` line 69, T-032 note; tbd
`is-01m32dz7yg…`): R012 gained `1.7e-5` on a fixed measure by changing only the selector
and the parent-centre restriction, Mira reports a failed attempt at `4.615` on the
unrestricted test, and nobody has measured what the selector and the restriction are
worth against a measure priced for them.

**Partly answered.** R038 is a point measure re-priced under the restriction
("parent-aware weight exchange") and it bought `+0.0011` over R012. So the restriction
is worth at least `+0.0011` when priced for, in the point language, on Mira’s support.
That part of the experiment no longer needs running.

**Not answered, and now more valuable.** The `+0.0056` from R038 to Kleddamag confounds
three changes: 2.7× more sites, virtual sites through near-coincident triples, and a
finer adaptive catalogue.
Whether the restriction is load-bearing on *that* support, and whether the triples are
necessary at all, is not measured.
The R012 review’s E5 (review lines 167–172) shows the restriction was load-bearing on
R012’s measure — swept unrestricted, entries 0 and 1 fall to `0.9875` and `0.9852`,
below `gamma` — but that is one measure at `A = 0.99999`.

The experiment is a 2×2 on Kleddamag’s 8,988 sites at `L = 4613/1000`:

| Cell | Value | Instrument | Runnable now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| restricted, point + 2-of-3 | `< 17` at `A = 0.99853` (the artifact) | — | known |
| restricted, point only | not measured | a row generator over parent-centre envelopes with per-row `(t, B)`: the row side of the C4 instrument | no |
| unrestricted, point only, `B` swept | not measured | `devtools.run_fractional_colgen --seed` on the 1440 net; Condition 4 admits `B < 0.999712` there | **yes** |
| unrestricted, point + 2-of-3, verify only | not measured | translate the measure into a T-025-shaped record, `decide_threshold_certificate` at `B = 0.998367`, 1440 net | **yes**, as a verifier run; re-pricing needs the production 2-of-3 pricer |

Two cells run on stock instruments today, and the unrestricted cells are one-sidedly
decisive: an unrestricted covering below 17 implies a restricted one, so a pass there
says the restriction is not load-bearing on this support and hands the repository a
first-party certificate in its own gate; a fail measures, by its least charge, what the
restriction carries.
That is why the experiment survives, reshaped: it is no longer a question about R012’s
selector, it is the cheapest way to find out whether the next n=17 rung is bought by
sites (colgen, which exists) or by the restricted language and relational atoms (a
producer, which does not).

What it would no longer buy: a demonstration that the restriction can be priced for
(R038), or that the T-025 atom runs at n=17 (the artifact).
What it still buys: the split of the `+0.0056`, a first-party n=17 result if the
unrestricted cell passes, and the calibration the register already asks for —
`RESULTS.md` line 58 records that the n=17 covering value is set “by the site set at
least as much as by the side”, and the repository’s own site sets at `461/100` reached
only `17.195968` unconverged (`covering-values.yaml`, reach table line 64) where Mira’s
1,620 atoms cover at `4.613`.

## Question 2: What the n=17 Threshold Result Implies for n=11

Three measured facts, then the verdict.

1. **The gain is already booked at n=11, in a different regime.** T-025 carries
   `2.293641` of threshold budget against `10.967323` total (`results.yaml` lines
   1352–1358), a 20.9% share against the artifact’s 13.9%. But T-025’s triples are wide
   (median `0.534`) and encode a relation between parts of the square; the artifact’s
   are near-coincident (median `0.014`) and encode a virtual site.
   The n=17 result is therefore evidence that *support enrichment* buys a lot at n=17;
   it is not evidence that relational atoms do.

2. **No n=11 certificate of any language has passed the one-body ceiling in
   exact-orientation terms.** The universal point ceiling is `L* = 38200/9977 =
   3.828806` (X-040 lines 78–80), which is exactly T-025’s own `L/B` on the 181 net.
   The frozen T-025 atoms do not cover at `B = 9977/10000` on any finer net (least
   charge `0.9749` at 360, 720 and 1440 steps; lane-a2 table rows 4–5,
   `results/agenda-034/lane-a2-threshold-certificate-on-finer-nets.md` lines 60–125);
   their crossing shrink is `B* = 249507/250000` at both 720 and 1440 with the same
   binding direction `29.46°` (rows 8, 11–12), so the atoms’ exact-orientation limit is
   `L/B* = 3.827548`, and T-026’s `3.826447` is `0.0011` below it.
   X-040’s D4 figure of `0.0024` (line 159) used `L*` as the target; the honest cap for
   the frozen atoms is `0.0011`. The A6 64-family plateau at `153/40` is
   `L/B = 3.833820` and is a site-set negative, not a wall (X-037 lines 99–100, 273).

3. **The n=11 measure was priced once, at the 181 net, and only re-verified since.**
   Lane-a2 F-A: the frozen threshold atoms lose 2.5% of least charge when the net
   doubles. Nobody has re-priced the n=11 threshold measure at a fine net or catalogue,
   with more sites, at a `B` below `9977/10000`. That is the exact analogue of the step
   the artifact took at n=17, and it is untried.

**Verdict.** There is no measured reason to expect `+0.0067` at n=11 from 2-of-3 atoms
as such, because the n=17 atoms are virtual sites and n=11 already spends more of its
budget on genuine relations.
There is a measured reason to expect *something* from the recipe: re-pricing T-025’s
language on a fine net with an enriched support has a ceiling of `+0.0013` to `L*` and,
if it passes `L*`, up to `+0.006` to the A6 plateau, and passing `L*` would be the first
n=11 certificate of any kind above the one-body ceiling in exact-orientation terms — a
new fact in kind, not a rung.
H-232’s `5/4` against budget 1 (chunk-5 record lines 53–55) is a per-atom overshoot
inside one conditioned class and says nothing about the global figure.
