# H-215 — which animation kinetics predict Search quality

Smoothness, contact persistence and neighbour gaps are mechanism measurements.
They do not establish that a run is a packing or that it improved the objective.
A later round can join these measurements to independently admitted Search receipts
under equal work, then test whether any relationship survives held-out seed blocks.

The question remains blocked until the kinetics command and the stable preset contract
land. It has no criterion because no direction is assumed in advance.
