# Exp-214 n=13 399/100 Covering Receipt

Status: **unresolved**. Neither confirmation nor refutation.

This run is calibration of the point-atom language one hundredth below a proved integer.
It is not a bound, and it does not move Bentz 2010’s `s(13) = 4`.

[H-223](../../../../hypotheses/H-223-n13-point-covering-at-399-100.md) asks whether a
point-atom certificate exists at `(n, L, B, net) = (13, 399/100, 9977/10000, 181
directions)` on a named site set including the ceiling-window lattice.
Confirm only with a frozen covering of mass strictly below 13 that both routes of
`devtools.decide_certificate` accept.
Refute at this scope only with an exact depth-one family of total at least 13 that
`devtools.independent_ceiling_reader` and `verify_ceiling` both accept.

The restricted optimum converged at `15.565562222801985`, which is a float LP objective
and not a bound. The polished dual family has exact total `85/8`. Neither side of the
criterion is reached, so the claim stands open and only this site set is refuted.

## Command

[exp-214](../../experiments/exp-214-h223-n13-399-100-window-covering.md):

  --log campaign/series/series-000-smoke-and-calibration/results/agenda-040/exp-214-n13-399-100.log

This is the same guard the [h216 receipt](../agenda-037/h216-n6-299-100-receipt.md)
handled, and the duplicates are the same kind: eight placements were the two writings of
one upright square, `t = 0` and `t = 1` at the same centre and side.

Folding `t = 1` to `t = 0` and summing weights left 312 placements and preserved the
total exactly at `298314/19165`; `placement_orbits` then accepted the record.
The merge changed nothing else, the merged bytes are retained beside the raw family, and
the fold is now the retained tool `devtools.fold_ceiling_family`, whose
`--check exp-214-n13-399-100-family-merged.json` re-folds the raw family to exactly

## Determination

H-223 remains **unresolved**, and only this site set is refuted.

- Confirmation failed: the restricted optimum converged at `15.565562222801985` and the
  frozen covering carries mass `15565619/1000000`, so no freeze below 13 exists to
  decide. `decide_certificate` was not invoked, and nothing here prints `RETAINABLE`.
- Refutation failed: the exact depth-one family both readers accept has total `85/8`,
  which is not at least 13, so the claim is not killed at this scope either.
- Under the record’s criterion a converged restricted optimum at or above 13 refutes the
  named site set only.
  That is what happened: auto grids `(26, 35, 44)` plus the 5-per-window lattice, 548
  orbits over 4053 sites, cannot carry a point-atom certificate at `399/100`.
- Weak duality on this support gives every D4-symmetric covering measure mass at least
  `85/8`. That is a language-wide statement well below the threshold, not a
  determination.
- The float objective `15.565562222801985` is a float LP value on one restricted site
  set. It is not a bound and is not used as one.

Calibration reading: the shrunk grid ceiling `3.9908` clears `3.99` by `0.0008`, so a
certificate at this side is not excluded by the ceiling, but the first window-seeded
site set overshoots 13 by more than 2.5. The endpoint case at `m = 4` is not cheap in
this language.
